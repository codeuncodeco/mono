Questions, answered

Can I change plans later?

Any time. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades at the end of the billing month. Your manuscripts never notice.

What happens to my work if I cancel?

You get a complete export in every format we support, and we keep a read-only copy for 90 days in case you change your mind.

Do you offer student discounts?

Yes — the Author plan is half price with a student email address. Theses count as manuscripts; we checked.
